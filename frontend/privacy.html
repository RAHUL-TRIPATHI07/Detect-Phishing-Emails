Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Sentinel AI ("Sentinel", "we", "us", or "our") is an email security analysis application designed to help users identify potential spam and phishing threats in their Gmail mailbox.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sentinel AI accesses, how that information is used, and how users can control their information.

1. Information We Access

When you connect a Gmail account, Sentinel AI requests read-only access to your Gmail mailbox through Google's Gmail API.

This may include:

  • Email subjects
  • Email message content
  • Email metadata required to retrieve and analyze messages
  • Your Gmail account email address

Sentinel AI does not request permission to send, modify, delete, or manage your Gmail messages.

2. How We Use Gmail Data

Gmail data is used solely to provide Sentinel AI's email security analysis functionality.

Sentinel analyzes email content using two independent machine-learning models:

  • Spam detection
  • Phishing detection

The resulting signals are combined through the Sentinel risk engine to produce a security assessment.

Sentinel AI does not use Gmail content for advertising and does not sell Gmail data to third parties.

3. Gmail Permissions

Sentinel AI uses Google's gmail.readonly permission.

This permission allows Sentinel AI to read Gmail messages required for analysis. It does not allow Sentinel AI to:

  • Send emails
  • Delete emails
  • Modify emails
  • Change Gmail settings
  • Manage contacts
  • Perform other actions within Gmail on your behalf

You can revoke Sentinel AI's access to your Google account through your Google Account security settings.

4. Authentication and OAuth

Gmail authentication is handled through Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization system.

Sentinel AI does not receive or store your Google account password.

After authorization, Sentinel AI receives authorization credentials that allow the application to access the Gmail API on your behalf.

OAuth-related information is used to maintain the authorized connection and provide the requested Gmail analysis functionality.

5. Email Analysis

Email content retrieved from Gmail is processed by Sentinel AI's backend services to generate spam and phishing predictions.

The application uses machine-learning models to calculate security signals and a risk engine to produce a final classification.

6. Data Storage

Sentinel AI may store information necessary to maintain an authenticated application session and Gmail connection.

Email content is retrieved from Gmail for the purpose of performing the requested security analysis.

Sentinel AI does not sell, rent, or use Gmail content for advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

Sentinel AI relies on third-party services necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Google OAuth and the Gmail API for authentication and mailbox access
  • Hosting and infrastructure services used to operate Sentinel AI

These services may process information according to their respective policies and terms.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to protect credentials and information handled by Sentinel AI.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

You may stop using Sentinel AI and revoke its Gmail access at any time through your Google Account.

If you want application-related information associated with your Sentinel AI connection to be removed, you may contact us using the contact information provided by the application.

10. Children's Privacy

Sentinel AI is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently use online services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the Sentinel AI project administrator through the contact information provided with the application.
